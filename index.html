📖 What is This Platform?

This platform is a cybersecurity learning roadmap designed for Bilkent University CTIS (Computer Technology and Information Systems) department students. CTIS curriculum courses are mapped to hands-on platforms like HTB Academy and TryHackMe. This allows you to reinforce your theoretical knowledge in practical lab environments.
